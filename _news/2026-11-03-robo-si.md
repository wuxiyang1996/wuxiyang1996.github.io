We publish a [results site](https://wuxiyang1996.github.io/robo-si-results/) for robo-si, our benchmark of embodied tasks on self-modeling, tool discovery, loaded locomotion and multi-robot cooperation. It lists 15 accepted task families and 158 configurations across six robot platforms, each with its conditions, tools, embodiment, refusal cases and a sped-up reference video.
