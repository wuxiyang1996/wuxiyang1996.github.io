We release a [technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18082) introducing BELIEFWEAVER, a framework for failure recovery in long-horizon agents. It combines persistent belief updates with implicit world modeling to guide repair attempts, and distills the recurring reasoning into a 9B student to reduce teacher inference cost.
